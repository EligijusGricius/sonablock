Specifications

Fasteners

Due to potential fire safety requirements of the building insurance company, as well as possible thermal and moisture-related influences, we recommend continuous metal fastening that mechanically connects all layers of the panels to the concrete ceiling. The selection and type of fastening depend on the substrate and the construction.

Dehydration

The panels should not be exposed to rapid drying of the building structure using cold and warm air blowers or high-performance dehumidifiers.

Plaster

Sonablock® acoustic panels are designed for modern architectural and interior applications, offering flexible integration across commercial, public, and residential environments. For projects involving additional surface treatments, coatings, or custom finishing solutions, all application methods must follow the respective manufacturer's technical guidelines and installation recommendations. Compatibility, adhesion performance, and long-term durability should always be verified prior to application. Application of building physics

The applications of our products can vary depending on the building, its construction, room usage, and other framework conditions or building physics requirements. We therefore recommend that you have the intended use and suitability of the products for building physics assessed beforehand by our staff or qualified experts in building physics, energy, and acoustics.

Preparatory work for laying

The original packaging offers no protection against the elements. The goods must be protected from moisture on the construction site and stored properly. To minimize dimensional changes caused by temperature or humidity, such as shrinkage or swelling of the panels, we recommend storing the panels uncovered for at least 7 days under the later climatic conditions (acclimatization).

Since wood wool has a high capacity for absorbing moisture, minor dimensional changes are possible even after installation. This is a normal physical reaction and not a defect, therefore no warranty claims can be made. Panels that have warped due to thermal or moisture-related influences should be stored with the sides facing the other way. If the panels do not return to their normal state, please do not use them and consult our sales advisors.

UNITEX multi-layer insulation panels and UNICEM lightweight panels can be easily processed or cut to size using the machines or tools commonly used for wood-based materials.

Surface coatings

UNITEX KD Type 2 and UNITEX Type 2 multi-layer insulation boards are available ex-works with various special surface finishes. All UNITEX multi-layer insulation boards and UNICEM lightweight panels are also available in RAL or NCS colors.

Fire protection

The fire protection requirements for insulation boards can vary depending on the type and height of the building, its use, and other regulations or cantonal requirements. In addition to the fire behavior of the insulation material and the facing layer, the choice of fastening material is also crucial for approval. We therefore recommend a preliminary assessment.

Advice

If you have any questions or uncertainties regarding our products and their processing, as well as solutions for special designs and applications, our staff will be happy to advise you.
