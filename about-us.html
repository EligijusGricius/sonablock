Stiga RM + Sonablock" story

Sonablock® is built on the vertically integrated infrastructure of Stiga RM — a Northern European manufacturing group with deep roots in forestry, engineering, transportation, and advanced wood processing.

From responsibly managed Latvian forests and raw material preparation to in-house metalworking, automated production technologies, logistics, and quality control, every stage of the process is connected within one ecosystem.

This integrated approach allows Sonablock® to maintain exceptional quality consistency, production precision, sustainable resource management, and reliable acoustic panel performance across projects of every scale.

Every Sonablock® panel begins its journey in the sustainably managed forests of Latvia, where we source high-quality wood fibers that form the core of our acoustic solutions.

Total Game Changer for my Home Studio

Produced in the world’s most advanced acoustic panel factories, Sonablock combines design and performance to deliver high-quality acoustic solutions that enhance sound absorption, improve safety, and create healthier indoor environments. We create acoustic panels for spaces that demand more — more character, more comfort, and better performance. By combining cutting-edge production with refined design, Sonablock transforms sound into part of the architectural experience.

At Sonablock, we believe acoustic comfort should feel as good as it looks. Every panel is developed with precision manufacturing, responsible materials, and contemporary interiors in mind — bringing together performance, aesthetics, and durability in one seamless solution.

Built for modern interiors and conscious projects, our panels are made to reduce noise, elevate atmosphere, and bring lasting quality into every space.

From workplaces and hospitality spaces to modern homes, our products are designed to create calmer, clearer, and more inspiring environments through intelligent acoustic design.
