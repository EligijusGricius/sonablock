CHOOSE YOUR FIBER TYPE

Sonablock by Stiga RM acoustic panels are manufactured from high-quality mineralized spruce wood fibers, bonded with either white or grey Portland cement. Depending on design preferences and acoustic requirements, we offer three different wood fiber structures.
